Privacy Policy - CameraFiltros

Last updated: November 2026

This web application uses the Snap Camera Kit SDK to apply Augmented Reality (AR) lenses and masks in real time via your device's camera.

Camera and Data Usage

All camera video processing occurs locally in your browser through Snap's AR engine. I do not collect, store, record, or transmit any facial data, video feeds, or personal information to external servers.

Third-Party Services

This application integrates Snap Inc.'s Camera Kit. For details on how Snap processes AR lens requests, please refer to Snap's Privacy Policy.
